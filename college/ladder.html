Why only three rungs (not sharp-scout's four)

Ceiling and Sharp port straight over. The other two cannot, and faking them would be theatre:
  • Form is dropped. sharp-scout re-weights recent records because betting-scout models on records. This bot deliberately doesn't — FPI/BPI is already strength-of-schedule adjusted and regressed. There is no record here to re-weight, and injecting one is the exact mistake this bot was built to avoid.
  • Clean became Fresh. A roster freeze needs a transaction wire, and college has none — ESPN reports 0 transactions for NCAAF and NCAAB, because college has no trades. Injuries are no substitute either: 27 across 23 NCAAF teams (MLB has ~290), and NCAAB reports zero. So Fresh keeps the purpose — "my model's inputs don't describe the current team" — using what college actually has: stale ratings and preseason priors.

📡 Ratings freshness — what the Fresh rung reads

ESPN serves the newest index it has published. In the gap before a new season goes live that is last season's, and every rating on the board then describes last year's roster. Fresh also sits out early season, when FPI/BPI is mostly preseason prior rather than results (min games: NCAAF 4, NCAAB 8 — that counts games played, not win% — sample size, never performance).
