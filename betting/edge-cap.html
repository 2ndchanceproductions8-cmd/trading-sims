Playbook

The humility ceiling. The Careful book with exactly one change: an edge above +10% is refused instead of backed. Same gate function, one added bound, so the two books cannot drift apart on anything else. Why: any book that ranks by edge under a three-a-day cap ends up backing its three most extreme disagreements with the market every single day — and fleet-wide the 10%+ bucket returned −0.091 R over 86 bets, the worst of any bucket. A large disagreement with a price is far more often a modelling error than an insight. This was the only sports bot in the fleet without a ceiling book, while the ceiling is the one intervention with repeated independent support here: totals-scout's edge_ceiling is one of only two robustly significant gate results the fleet has produced (−0.251, t = −2.62), sharp-scout's Ceiling is its best rung, and spread-scout's sits near the top of its ladder. The +10% matches sharp-scout's bar deliberately — a different number would make this a second variable against the evidence that motivated it. Honest limit: this is gate-tuning, and gate-tuning cannot repair a signal that measures zero — across 282 settled bets the correlation between edge and result was −0.047. It bounds the damage from a bad model; it does not make the model good.
