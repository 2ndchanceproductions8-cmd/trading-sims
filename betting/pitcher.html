Playbook

The starting-pitcher rung. This is the Careful book with exactly one change: in MLB the model probability is adjusted for the starting-pitcher matchup. Every other gate is literally Careful's — both books call the same gate function — and outside MLB, or when a starter isn't listed, the adjustment is zero and this book is bit-for-bit the control. Why it exists: across 282 settled bets the correlation between this fleet's model edge and the actual result was −0.047 — zero, if anything backwards, with the highest-confidence bets performing worst. That is a model with no information, and no gate or stake change can repair it; the input has to change. In MLB the starter is the single largest determinant of a game and the control ignores him completely, while the market plainly does not. ERA comes from the same ESPN payload the bot already fetches, shrunk toward the day's own slate average by the pitcher's decisions so a hot three-start line doesn't read as an ace. This may still not beat the close — the market knows the pitcher too — which is exactly why it is a parallel book judged on CLV rather than an edit to the control.
